# Northwind Solar knowledge base for the blog writer

This is an example project. Northwind Solar is a fictional company, and every fact, figure, client and quote below is invented to show how a finished knowledge base looks. Replace it with your own.

## 1. Who Northwind Solar is

- **What we do:** Northwind Solar designs and installs solar panels and home batteries for homes and small businesses.
- **Where we work:** Bristol, Bath and within 40 miles of our Avonmouth depot.
- **Since:** 2014. A team of 18, including 9 qualified installers.
- **Contact:** 0117 000 0000, hello@northwind-solar.example, Unit 4, Avonmouth Trade Park, Bristol BS11 0XX.
- **Facts and figures we can quote:**
  - 1,200 home installations since 2014.
  - Every quote follows a free roof survey in person, never a satellite estimate alone.
  - Average install time for a 10-panel home system: one day, with scaffolding up the day before.

## 2. Services (what we can honestly offer)

### Homes
- **Solar panel installation:** survey, design, scaffolding, installation, inverter, grid notification and handover. 6 to 20 panels for most homes.
- **Battery storage:** 5 kWh and 10 kWh batteries, added with new panels or to an existing system.
- **EV charger installation:** 7 kW home chargers, installed on their own or with solar.

### Businesses
- **Commercial rooftop solar:** systems from 20 to 150 panels for offices, workshops and farms.

### After installation
- **Annual health check:** panel clean, inverter check and a written performance report.
- **Repairs:** we repair systems we installed. We do not repair other installers' systems.

- Every quote follows an in-person roof survey.

## 4. Claim rules (hard rules: never break)

- **Prices:** Northwind Solar never publishes prices; costs come only in a personal quote after a survey.
- **Savings:** never guarantee savings or payback times. Explain the factors that decide them.
- **Certifications:** say "MCS certified installer". Never "MCS approved" or "MCS accredited".
- **Ratings:** never quote star ratings or review counts.
- **Superlatives:** no "best", "number one" or "cheapest".
- **Statistics:** only the figures in section 1, or third-party figures with a named source and year.
- **Competitors:** never name or criticise one.
- **Repairs:** we only repair systems we installed. Never suggest we repair other installers' systems.

## 5. Voice and tone

A warm, grounded expert: someone who has been on hundreds of roofs and tells the reader plainly what works. Confident, practical and specific. Never jargon, hype or PR gloss.

- **Answer first.** Open with the direct answer to the title.
- **Every paragraph earns its place:** a fact, an explanation, an example or an action.
- **Guidance first, proof second:** practical advice any homeowner can use, then our first-hand detail as evidence.
- Address the reader as "you"; the company is "we", "our installers" or "Northwind Solar".
- Be honest when a smaller system, or no battery, is the better choice.

## 6. Spelling, vocabulary and mechanics

- **Language:** UK English. Currency: £.
- **Preferred words:**

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| solar panels | solar cells (for the whole panel) |
| install | fit |
| battery storage | power bank |
| inverter | inverter box |

- **Acronyms written exactly as:** PV, MCS, EPC, kWh, kW.
- **Units and dates:** "4 kWh", "6.6 kW", "15 October 2026".

## 7. Headings and casing

Sentence case for the H1 and every heading below it. Acronyms as in section 6.

## 8. Post anatomy

The workflow's own structure applies. Our posts always end with what to send for a quote: property type, roof size and direction, yearly electricity use and postcode.

## 9. Link rules

- One homepage link in the opening, a quote or survey link in the closing, 5 to 8 internal links in total.
- Link a case study only where it proves the point being made.

## 10. Metadata

- Meta title: "Primary keyword | Secondary keyword | Northwind Solar".
- Blog categories: Solar guides, Costs and savings, Installation tips.
