## 5. Voice and tone

A warm, grounded expert: someone with years of hands-on experience who tells the reader plainly what works. Confident, practical and specific. Never corporate jargon, hype or PR gloss.

- **Answer first.** Open with the direct answer to the title, never with a scene, a definition or "In today's world".
- **Every paragraph earns its place:** a fact, an explanation, an example or an action.
- **Guidance first, proof second:** practical advice any reader can use, then our first-hand detail as evidence.
- Address the reader as "you"; the company is "we", "our team" or "[Brand]".
